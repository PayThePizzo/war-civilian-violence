How does violence change over time?

Tracks weekly counts of state-based, non-state, and one-sided events together with the one-sided share and civilian fatality toll, so shifts in the mix of violence over the conflict are visible at a glance.

02 / WHERE?

Where does violence concentrate?

Aggregates events into H3 hexagons across Sudan, showing where combat and one-sided violence intensify and cluster geographically, for the full period or a single week.

03 / WHO?

How do actors differ?

Compares armed actors across six metrics - combat involvement, losses suffered, one-sided violence against civilians, and geographic reach - to profile distinct patterns of behavior.

04 / BEFORE & AFTER

What surrounds peaks of one-sided violence?

Examines combat activity and fatalities in the weeks before and after each actor's worst episodes of violence against civilians, to describe temporal association - not to claim cause.

Source: UCDP Georeferenced Event Dataset. These views describe associations, not causal effects.
