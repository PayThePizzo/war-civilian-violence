# Phase 4 - Tangible design: tangible representations for public engagement

Proposal of a tangible representation of the dataset selected in Phase 1 (or a part of it), taking advantage of the toolkit already used in the classroom. Please check the abacus of all the components available in Moodle.

You can decide freely which components to use, but the final representation must also be accessible to non-sighted users, permitting them to discriminate the different variables and their values in time and space. Please check the guide available in Moodle.

Deliverable:

A report containing:

- sketches of the tangible representations (the final version of the physical representations will be created during the exam sessions)
- a copy of the subset of the dataset used for the representation (just point to data)
- a description of the design choices, including a description of the mapping choices and the knowledge that is expected to stem from the examination of the representations

The tangible representation will be judged for its accessibility, clarity, and capability to communicate a message, its capability to outline interesting correlations between the different variables, its capability to engage people, and the exploitation of the available tangible components for visualizing a relevant number of variables.
