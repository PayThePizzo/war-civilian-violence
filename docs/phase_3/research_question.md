# Research Question

## Main question

When and where do armed actors shift from direct combat toward deliberate violence against civilians, and what temporal, spatial, and actor-specific patterns accompany this transition?

## Methodological caution

This project is descriptive and exploratory. It is intended to identify and characterize temporal, spatial, and actor-specific patterns in the available data, rather than to establish causal effects. In particular, the analysis should not claim that battlefield losses cause violence against civilians. Any association between battlefield outcomes and civilian targeting should be presented as a pattern requiring further investigation, not as evidence of causation.
